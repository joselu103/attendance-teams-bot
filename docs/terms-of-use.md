# Attendance CRMT Development Bot Terms of Use

**Effective date:** 2026-08-26

## Development-only service

Attendance CRMT (Development) is a development-only Microsoft Teams bot. It is
provided solely to test Microsoft Teams, Azure Bot Service, and bot-hosting
connectivity. It is not approved for production attendance management.

## No attendance actions or decisions

At the effective date, the bot does not retrieve, create, update, approve, or
otherwise manage attendance records. Its responses must not be treated as
attendance data, an employment record, authorization, or a business decision.

## Acceptable use

Use the bot only with an authorized development account and only for harmless
connectivity testing. Do not submit credentials, access tokens, employee data,
attendance information, or other sensitive or personal information.

## Availability and changes

The bot may be changed, restarted, disabled, or removed without notice. It is
provided as-is for development testing and may return incomplete, fixed, or
non-authoritative responses.

## Responsibility

The Attendance CRMT system remains the authoritative boundary for attendance
business rules, authorization, audit, and data access. This development bot does
not replace that system.

## Changes and contact

These terms may change as the bot gains functionality. Questions about this
development bot or these terms should be directed to the Attendance CRMT project
maintainer.
